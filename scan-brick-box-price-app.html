How to Scan a Brick Set Box and See Its Price on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the BrickWorth developer

Short answer. Open BrickWorth, tap Scan a set and photograph the front of the box. It reads the set and shows the retail price plus an estimated value for sealed, built or loose. Photo identification is part of Pro, which starts with a 3-day free trial; searching by number is free.

Step by step

  1. Tap Scan a setThe camera opens with a frame for the box.
  2. Take the photoPhotograph the front of the box in good light, or choose a photo from your library.
  3. Review the resultSee the set number, name, year, pieces, minifigures and retail price.
  4. Check the valueSwitch between Sealed, Built and Used for the estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does scanning need internet?

Yes. Set data comes from public set catalogs through our server, and photos are used only for identification and are not stored.

What if the scan picks the wrong set?

Type the set number under Know the number? to look it up directly.

Is scanning free?

Photo identification is part of BrickWorth Pro, with a 3-day free trial. Searching sets is free.

What does BrickWorth do?

It identifies a brick set from a photo of the box, the built model or the instruction booklet, then shows its details, retail price and an estimated value, and lets you track your collection on a shelf.

Is the value a real market price?

No. It is a modeled estimate based on the original retail price and the time since the set retired, shown for sealed, built complete and used loose conditions. It is not an appraisal and not a record of recent sales. For a sale price, check recent sold listings on a marketplace.

Is BrickWorth free?

Searching sets, seeing retail price and retired status, and keeping sets on your shelf are free. Photo identification and value estimates are part of BrickWorth Pro.
