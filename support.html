BrickWorth — Support

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Need help with BrickWorth? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — we usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How is the value calculated? From the official US retail price and how many years ago the set retired. Sets still on sale sit a little below retail; retired sets gain value over time. Every value is marked as an estimate.

A scan didn't find my set. Photograph the front of the box in good light, or type the set number printed on the box.

Restore my subscription. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Cancel my subscription. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BrickWorth.

Where is my data? Only on your device. There's no account.
