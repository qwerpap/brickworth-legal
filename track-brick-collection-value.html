How to Track the Value of Your Brick Collection on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the BrickWorth developer

Short answer. Add each set to your BrickWorth shelf and it totals the estimated value of your collection, shows how it changed over time and compares it with what you paid. Export the shelf as CSV. No account is needed.

Step by step

  1. Add setsScan a box or search by number and tap Add to shelf.
  2. Enter what you paidCompare the estimate with your purchase price.
  3. Watch the totalThe shelf shows total estimated value and the change over time.
  4. ExportExport your collection as a CSV file.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No, there is no sign-in.

Can I keep a wishlist?

Yes, the Wishlist tab saves sets you want next.

Is there a limit on sets?

Free users can keep sets on the shelf; an unlimited shelf and wishlist are part of Pro.

What does BrickWorth do?

It identifies a brick set from a photo of the box, the built model or the instruction booklet, then shows its details, retail price and an estimated value, and lets you track your collection on a shelf.

Is the value a real market price?

No. It is a modeled estimate based on the original retail price and the time since the set retired, shown for sealed, built complete and used loose conditions. It is not an appraisal and not a record of recent sales. For a sale price, check recent sold listings on a marketplace.

Is BrickWorth free?

Searching sets, seeing retail price and retired status, and keeping sets on your shelf are free. Photo identification and value estimates are part of BrickWorth Pro.
