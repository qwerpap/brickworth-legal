BrickWorth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

BrickWorth is made by Danil Chshavelev. This policy explains what the app handles and why. Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your collection stays on your device.

Your collection

The sets on your shelf and wishlist, their condition, quantities and the prices you paid are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos you scan

When you scan a set, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to OpenAI to read the set number and name. The photo is used only to answer that request and is not stored by us. OpenAI processes it under its API data policy, which does not use API data to train models.

Set lookups

Searches and set numbers you look up are sent to our server, which queries the public Rebrickable and Brickset catalogues. Set data is cached on the server; your queries are not linked to you.

Install identifier

The app creates a random identifier on first launch and sends it with scan requests, only to apply a daily scan limit. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, device ID or any other data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

What we don't do

No analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale or sharing of personal data.

Service providers

Google Cloud / Firebase (hosts our server), OpenAI (photo reading), Rebrickable and Brickset (public set catalogues).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
