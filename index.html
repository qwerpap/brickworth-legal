BrickWorth Get the App

Minifig Collector & Value

BrickWorth is an app that finds out what your brick sets are worth. Take a photo of a set box, a built model or an instruction booklet, and BrickWorth identifies the set (number, name, year, piece count, minifigures with photos, retired or not) and shows its retail price and an estimated value as sealed, built complete or used and loose. Add sets to your shelf to track your collection's value over time against what you paid.

What BrickWorth does

  • Scan a set box, model or instruction bookletTake a photo or pick one from your library. BrickWorth reads the set and shows its number, name, year, piece count and minifigures with photos.
  • Retail price and retired statusSee the original retail price and whether the set is retired. Searching sets by number or name, retail price and retired status are free.
  • Estimated value: sealed, built or loosePick the condition and see a modeled estimate for a sealed box, a complete build or used loose bricks. It is an estimate built from retail price and time since retirement, not a record of market sales.
  • A shelf for your collectionAdd sets to your shelf and track the total estimated value and its change over time against what you paid.
  • Wishlist and themesKeep a wishlist of sets you want next and browse sets by theme.
  • CSV exportTake your collection with you as a CSV file.

Frequently asked questions

What does BrickWorth do?

It identifies a brick set from a photo of the box, the built model or the instruction booklet, then shows its details, retail price and an estimated value, and lets you track your collection on a shelf.

Is the value a real market price?

No. It is a modeled estimate based on the original retail price and the time since the set retired, shown for sealed, built complete and used loose conditions. It is not an appraisal and not a record of recent sales. For a sale price, check recent sold listings on a marketplace.

Is BrickWorth free?

Searching sets, seeing retail price and retired status, and keeping sets on your shelf are free. Photo identification and value estimates are part of BrickWorth Pro.

How much does BrickWorth Pro cost?

$7.99 per month with a 3-day free trial, or $39.99 per year with a 3-day free trial. Both renew automatically until cancelled in Apple ID settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in anywhere in the app.

What if I do not have the box?

Type the set number, which is printed on the instruction booklet and the box, or search by name.

Does it identify loose bricks or single minifigures?

No. BrickWorth is about whole sets. It lists the minifigures included in a set but does not recognize loose parts from a pile. Apps like Brickit and BrickScan are built for that.

Is BrickWorth affiliated with the toy brand whose sets it covers?

No. BrickWorth is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by LEGO Group.
